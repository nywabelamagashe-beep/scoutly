Job hunting shouldn't be a second job.

You send application after application. Most get no reply, and you never find out why. Your CV might be the problem, or your targeting, or the timing. You can't tell, and meanwhile the weeks go by.

The Sprint fixes that. We handle the heavy lifting and show you what's happening at every step.

How it works

  1. Apply (2 minutes)

    Fill in the Interview-Readiness Scorecard. It scores your CV and target role, and you get your results even if you're not selected.

  2. Onboard (30 minutes)

    We verify your facts and rebuild your CV and answer bank, so every application is accurate and strong.

  3. Approve and send

    We find fit-scored roles and tailor each application. You approve the first 20. Your first 10 go out within 72 hours.

  4. Track and interview

    A weekly scorecard shows applications sent, replies, and interviews. When an interview lands, you get a prep call before it.

We'll also rebuild your CV and targeting with you. We can't promise a job, and we won't pretend to. We promise the work, and we'll keep doing it until you have interviews.

What we ask of you

It's free, but it isn't passive. We ask for:

  • A 30-minute onboarding
  • Your approval on your first 20 applications
  • A 10-minute weekly check-in, with honest reporting on replies and interviews
  • Permission to use your anonymized results, and a short testimonial if you get interviews
  • Two introductions to friends who'd benefit

If you go quiet, your spot goes to the next person on the waitlist.

It's for you if

  • You're actively looking for work and ready to be interviewed
  • You can give about 2 hours in total, plus a short weekly check-in
  • You'll report your results honestly

It's not for you if

  • You want a guaranteed job
  • You won't review what's sent in your name
  • You can't commit to the check-ins

Only 50 spots, because we can only serve about 10 people a week.

Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. You pay with effort, data, and honest feedback, not money.

What kind of jobs do you apply for?

Tell us the role you're targeting in the Scorecard. We only apply to roles that fit your experience and your goal.

What does "done-with-you" mean?

We do the searching, tailoring, and sending. You approve and give us the facts. Nothing goes out in your name that you haven't signed off on.

Will you send junk to employers?

No. Each application is fit-scored and tailored to the role. We cap volume on purpose.

What happens after the free month?

You choose. There's no obligation and no card required. Founding members keep their price for 12 months.

What happens to my data?

We use it only to run your search and, anonymized, to measure results. We handle it in line with POPIA.

What if I'm not selected?

You still get your Scorecard results, and we'll keep you on the waitlist.
